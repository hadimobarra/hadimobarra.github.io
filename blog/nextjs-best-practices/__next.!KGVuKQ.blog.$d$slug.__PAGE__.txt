<h2 id="6-check-what-your-bundler-merges">6. Check what your bundler merges</h2>
<p>I used <code>next/font</code> in both layouts, one font per language. The build merged their CSS into one chunk, and preloading works per chunk. Every English page preloaded the Persian fonts, and the other way around.</p>
<p>Nothing in the code looked wrong. The network panel showed it immediately. I ended up hosting the fonts myself, with an explicit preload per language. The <a href="/blog/less-javascript-same-blocking-time/">longer version</a> includes the wrong fix I tried first.</p>
