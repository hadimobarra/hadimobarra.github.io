About Me

Passionate about creating exceptional digital experiences

I'm a Front-End Engineer with 3+ years of production experience building complex, data-intensive web applications. I hold an M.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Iran University of Science and Technology and am recognised by the National Elite Foundation.

I'm an active contributor to OpenMetadata — a leading open-source data catalog and governance platform — with merged pull requests spanning UI components, localization, and feature enhancements. I thrive in agile, cross-functional teams and enjoy architecting scalable component libraries, driving performance optimisations (code-splitting, lazy loading, memoisation), and leading full RTL localisation efforts. Every project is an opportunity to push the boundaries of what's possible on the web.
