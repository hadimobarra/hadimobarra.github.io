About Me

Passionate about creating exceptional digital experiences

I'm a Front-End Engineer with 4+ years of production experience building complex, data-intensive web applications. I hold an M.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Iran University of Science and Technology and am a member of the National Elite Foundation.

I specialize in data-governance, data-catalog and ETL interfaces. I'm an upstream contributor to Apache Superset and have built custom features on OpenMetadata, including an ETL pipeline editor and full Persian (RTL) localization. I ship accessible, performant interfaces with React, TypeScript and modern state management.

I work well in agile, distributed teams and enjoy owning features end to end: architecting component libraries, optimizing performance (code-splitting, lazy loading, memoization) and leading i18n/RTL work. I'm open to remote full-time or contract roles and work in UTC+3:30, overlapping with European business hours.
